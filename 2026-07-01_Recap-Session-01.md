### Scene 1: Introductions

The party wake up in (what they later discover to be) the Inn above Crook's Nook Tavern with no knowledge of where they are, how they arrived there, or who the other party members are. It is early morning, and the building appears abandoned.
* Syl (Eric): Tiny fae who obsessively offers "bargains" to anyone who will listen. So far no takers.
* Kal (Eric): Corgi companion of Syl. Communicates telepathically using a voice as if from beyond. Flies.
* Hymars (Alan): Human. Frequently speaks in Polish when cursing to herself, which is canonically an unintelligible dialect of Taldane (Common). Very attached to her weapon of choice (war javelin).
* Izabella (Andy): Calls herself The Lady Izabella Zophiya Draga Red. Refers to most as peasants. Has severe mommy issues.
* Pen Channas (Dan): Lackadaisical ancient elf wearing the vestiges of the Goddess Seranrae. Not particularly motivated to get out of bed. Seemed interested in delivering the town guard into salvation by encouraging her to quit her job. A survivor, she scrounges for old food scraps and picks up anything in the keep that is not nailed down (including a dead rat) - stuffing the items into a pillow case.
### Scene 2: Eviction

Marisha, a local town guard on her first assignment, interrupts the party to serve them an official notice of eviction for squatting in the Inn above the Tavern - they have until 10AM to vacate. Moreover, Marisha mentions that the Innkeeper expects compensation for their night's stay. Marisha is terrified by Izabella Zohphiya, and Pen seemingly convinces Marisha that she should quit.
### Scene 3: Meeting Al'Qalav

Al'Qalav (Al'), the spectral avatar of a Spiresworn elf, strolls into the room and addresses the party as if knowing them. Hymars throws a javelin impotently through his chest. No one takes Al' seriously (despite him being a fucking specter) until he mentions Izabela's mother. This triggers her badly but causes her to take notice. He claims to have been working with them for months but that the experiment in magic they have volunteered to be part of has caused memory loss.

Al' and the party do not communicate effectively. He is loquacious and has a tendency to preamble, which does not mesh well with the group's chaos. He mentions they are the "F Team." He claims there are magical ley lines that connect different places, times, and universes together, and that that magic energy tends to pool or "eddy" when important events on the cusp of happening stall out. They are meant to be catalysts that help important changes happen so the magical energy can flow again.

---

Marisha comes back into the room, stepping through Al', appearing unaware of his existence. She was browbeaten by her mean boss into staying with the force and needed to come back to obtain the party's forwarding addresses for the bill. Pen explains that the proper way to quit is no-call-no-show, and collects Marisha's badge into her pillowcase. Marisha expresses disgust that the police are protecting a tavern run by thieves and storms out, determined to be unemployed, with a renewed sense of dignity.

---

Finish conversation with Al', who goes on about the magical "eddies." Al' directs the party to the local fishery, where he believes they may find their purpose in jumping here. Hymars believes the party must stab someone called Eddie. Al' indicates that the party may get stuck at an eddy point if they fail to complete their mission. Al' dissaperates into thin air and the group takes their time looting loose items and Syl lockpicking chests before heading into town.
### Scene 4: Tamily Tanderveil's Quest

The party are in Otari, a small coastal fishing and logging village in the Inner Sea (pf2e Mediterranean) region of Golarion (pf2e Earth). After explaining to Andy what a fishery is, the party heads to the fishery where they meet Tamily. Tamily is a folksy halfling woman who turns out to be the proprietor. She seems surprised the party already knows she needs help, since she's only just posting flyers to recruit adventurers. She finds this quite auspicious. She enjoys jokes at the expense of her husband. She opens the fishery up as a place for games, food, and entertainment after official business hours at the fishery are done. The idea of a fishery - turned - tavern delights Hymars.

After hours, barrels full of salted fish are going missing each night. Tamily suspects "critters" - she is flummoxed that anyone in town would be stealing from her. She wants the party to get to the root of the problem and make it stop. She offered 100 gold pieces as reward. The party attempts to haggle her into an additional per diem but she wants them to get started with the option to potentially renegotiate for an additional time-based stipend if it looks like the mission may drag on for long.
