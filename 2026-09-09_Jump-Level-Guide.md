### Archetypes
The Free Archetype variant rules grant all PCs a bonus feat every even level that may only be spent on Archetype Feats. 

Under the normal rules, taking an archetype feat replaces a Class Feat, forcing the player to delay their character's main build progression to dabble in extra abilities outside their class. Under this alternative rule-set, you may take archetype feats without sacrificing Class Feats. This allows all PCs to grow more versatile at around the same rate, diversifying characters without upending balance.

Tables using the Free Archetype rule-set still abide by all other rules for archetypes. The prerequisites and fulfillment requirements (how many Archetype Feats you must take before you can take a new Dedication Feat for a different Archetype) stay the same.

* [Rules for Archetypes](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2127)
* [Master List of Archetypes](https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx)
	* **Multiclass** Archetypes are used to dual-class your characters (with restrictions on your secondary class). 
	* **Class** Archetypes grant extra abilities to existing classes. 
	* **Combat Style**, **Core**, and **Mystical** Archetypes are add combat and utility to your character similar to Class Archetypes. These Archetypes still have prerequisites, but they are not restricted by class, allowing more builds to capitalize on them.
	* Feel free to explore the other categories of Archetypes listed but know that some are designed for specific campaigns, have advanced requirements, or exist mostly for flavor.
* The free archetype feat is not just an extra class feat - you may not sacrifice your free archetype feat to take two class feats.
* Free Archetype Feats are use-it-or-los- it at time of level - just like regular feat slots, you can't sandbag empty slots to stockpile higher level feats later.

> [!NOTE] Sole exception: Dan's character may "sandbag" once to sidestep a conflict between the Free Archetype rule-set and Pen's Level 1 Ancestry ability. 

### Equipment
You should create a wish-list with a max budget of **435 GP** for equipment to help bring your load-outs to a level consummate to your new character levels. You won't start the beginning of next session with these items but should have them ready.

**Paizo Recommendations for Lvl 6 PCs** *(very rough guidelines, but used by Paizo to balance encounters)*:

| Recommended Item       | Item Lvl | Cost in GP | Description                                             |
| ---------------------- | -------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| +1 Weapon Potency Rune | 2        | 35         | Grants *+1 Item Bonus* to attack rolls with that weapon |
| Striking Rune          | 4        | 65         | Adds a damage die to damage rolls with that weapon      |
| +1 Armor Potency Rune  | 5        | 160        | Grants *+1 Item Bonus* to AC (and some saving throws)   |
| Skill Items            | 3-4      | 50-100     | Grants *+1 Item Bonus* to specific skill checks         |

> [!IMPORTANT] Casters don't generally need weapon potency or striking runes so their 4th and 5th level picks often go to a signature magical staff, rank 2 Wands, or utility items. You may only cast spells you already have learned (in prepared spells list or repertoire) from staves and wands, but you don't have to have the spells prepared for that day.
