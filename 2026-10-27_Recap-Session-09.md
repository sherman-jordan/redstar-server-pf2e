### Setting & History:

- You find yourselves in the year 4482 AR - the final year of Harchrist's Blockade defending the human settlements along the River Esk in the Hold of Belzken. At this time, the fortlike settlements are a part of the country of Lastwall, but are under heavy siege by an incursion of colonizing orcs.
- Your baseline knowledge of basic history of Golarion is clear on the outcome of the sieges - the orcs will eventually overcome the siege and push all humans back within Lastwall's modern borders. The region changes hands several times between orcs, hill giants, and occasionally ogres, but Lastwall never reclaims the region and their civilization and regional influence is permanently diminished.
- Izabella remembers reading in more depth about this period of history - particularly about the horrific last stand of Redlake Fort. Late into the siege of Redlake, completely cut off from their supply line, the human commanders resorted to feeding the troops with the flesh of their comrades. The act was so vile and the trauma so brutal that the region is allegedly still haunted by their spirits in the present day.

### Fort Morrine:

- The party arrives through the portal in a fortressed port-town in the first light of dawn. The air is dense with fog. Syl does not exit the portal with the rest of the group.
- You feel a haze that has been gripping your minds begin to lift. Vague memories are starting to come back but nothing specific. You each begin to remember meeting and working together with the other members of your party, lending some validity to Al'Qalav's claims, but you don't have clear specific memories of your time before Otari. **You recall that there was another member of the group that is currently missing, in addition to Syl.**
- Al' is waiting for you as you fully materialize into the new scene: 
	- (Rehash) discussion about the overall project, the fact that the only way out is through, that your only hope of returning home is to complete missions. 
	- Izabella promptly rejects this and bloviates about being royalty and demands to know where the party is on a map so she can fly home. 
	- Al' informs you that you are no longer in your own time period and returning home is  physically impossible. 
- Mihail exits the local tavern and questions why the party is in the streets past curfew. He quickly decides the party is obviously from out of town, doesn't know local ordinance, and he can't be fucked to care.
- Al' and Pen have an aside regarding Pen's great quest on behalf of Sarenrae. Al' informs her he has visited both past and future many times as a projection but does not know if she completes her quest. He consoles her that it could actually be a sign of her success that he doesn't know of some great calamity she was intended to prevent.
- The party enters the local tavern/ inn and attempts to rent a room at Izabella's insistence. Izabella unsuccessfully attempts to negotiate a better deal with the barkeeper. Pen helps cover the cost to avoid a headache. The party is introduced to Nicholas, the off-putting young Damphir hailed as Lastwall's secret ultimate weapon in the coming final conflict. He gives incel vibes and is smitten by Hymars. Hymars is attracted to Mihail.
- Mihail orders some low ranking men to draw a bath for Izabella in her room.
- Pen, Izabella, and Mihail go upstairs. Izabella takes a bath (curtain is drawn). Izabella thinks Pen is attracted to Mihail and attempts to wingman. Pen and Mihail both think Izabella is attracted to Pen. Pen and Izabella each realize that Redlake Fort is doomed and remember the recorded history of the region.
- Meanwhile, Nicholas and Hymars chat and Hymars continually attempts to intimidate him into giving away his secrets. Nicholas thinks this is flirting and is both scared and horny. He folds immediately. When Hymars expresses his disgust over Nicholas' blatant disregard for OpSec, he ushers her into a secret private room (broom closet) and finds the environment even more evocative. He explains he is an exceptionally talented mage and will single handedly end the standoff between orc and man through the power of his magic. He has been groomed from a young age to be a weapon, but has never been allowed to engage in direct combat, because he was considered too important to risk.
