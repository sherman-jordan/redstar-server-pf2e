### Scene 1 Elements of Chaos

- Each party member was visited by two primal energy orbs after their battle with CinderRat - one of Earth and one of Wind, which beleaguered them until each party member chose one to bestow a blessing:
	- **Hymars**, **Pen**, and **Izabella** chose *Blessing of the Earth*, which grants them a +2 bonus to  melee strikes for one turn within the next 24 hours, activated at the time of their choosing. **Izabella** actually didn't choose so much as defiantly swatted at the orbs and happened to touch the Earth orb.
	- **Syl** chose *Blessing of the Air*, which allows them to fly up to 40 ft in one action in the next 24 hours, activated at the time of their choosing. Since they already ride a flying corgi, this bonus was justifiably a bit of a let-down for **Syl**; but, it's not anyone's fault as floating orbs can't tell adventures what they do.
- **Pen** attempts to loot the corpse of the CinderRat, which dissolves into a pile of ash. She loots the ash.

### Scene 2 Soggy Crossroads

- The party begins to travel North, until they come to the entrance of the smelly room, which they refuse to enter, and head back towards the staircase they entered the floor from.

### Scene 3 Mermaid Fountain

- The party comes to a room with an intricately carved mermaid fountain, pouring exceptionally pure clear water into its basin. **Izabella** notices something shiny on the floor of the fountain and **Hymars** notices that there are gears beginning to move and surmises that the whole fountain houses a complex automated trap.
- **Hymars** attempts to disable the trap with her javelins, but is not able to
- **Pen** manages to disable the fountain / automaton entirely by throwing the dead rat she has been carrying around in her pillowcase into the mechanism driving the it. The dead rat's repulsive matted hair and skin squelched unpleasantly as it wrapped itself inexorably from the cogs and gears. 
- **Izabella** loots 2GP, 8SP, 28CP in coin from the basin

### Scene 4 Kobold Warren

- The party heads North into the great hall of the Kobold warren, except  and finds themselves intruding upon a union meeting of Kobold warriors. **Kerev** and **Trapmaster Travis** are not present and the warriors are unsure/ uneasy on how to treat the party.
- The Kobold warriors appear to have no idea how unions are meant to function. They are attempting to establish a charter of sorts but lack any understanding of the purpose of a union and have trouble codifying their structure because they also don't know how to run an effective meeting.
- **Syl** begins to lie and bargain with the union members
