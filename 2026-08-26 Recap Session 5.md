### Scene 1 Elements of Chaos

- Each party member was visited by two primal energy orbs after their battle with CinderRat - one of Earth and one of Wind, which beleaguered them until each party member chose one to bestow a blessing:
	- **Hymars**, **Pen**, and **Izabella** chose *Blessing of the Earth*, which grants them a +2 bonus to  melee strikes for one turn within the next 24 hours, activated at the time of their choosing. **Izabella** actually didn't choose so much as defiantly swatted at the orbs and happened to touch the Earth orb.
	- **Syl** chose *Blessing of the Air*, which allows them to fly up to 40 ft in one action in the next 24 hours, activated at the time of their choosing. Since they already ride a flying corgi, this bonus was justifiably a bit of a let-down for **Syl**; but, it's not anyone's fault as floating orbs can't tell adventures what they do.
- **Pen** attempts to loot the corpse of the CinderRat, which dissolves into a pile of ash. She loots the ash.

### Scene 2: Soggy Crossroads

- The party begins to travel North, until they come to the entrance of the smelly room, which they refuse to enter, and head back towards the staircase they entered the floor from.

### Scene 3: Mermaid Fountain

- The party comes to a room with an intricately carved mermaid fountain, pouring exceptionally pure clear water into its basin. **Izabella** notices something shiny on the floor of the fountain and **Hymars** notices that there are gears beginning to move and surmises that the whole fountain houses a complex automated trap.
- **Hymars** attempts to disable the trap with her javelins, but is not able to
- **Pen** manages to disable the fountain / automaton entirely by throwing the dead rat she has been carrying around in her pillowcase into the mechanism driving the it. The dead rat's repulsive matted hair and skin squelched unpleasantly as it wrapped itself inexorably from the cogs and gears. 
- **Izabella** loots 2GP, 8SP, 28CP in coin from the basin
- The bickering between **Pen** and **Izabella** comes to a head, after the latter calls the former a peasant. Because of this slight (among a multitude of other greivances), **Pen** shoves **Izabella** into the fountain when she leans over to loot the loose change, and **Izabella** retaliates by firing off multiple *Elemental Blast* attacks (and missing each time).

### Scene 4: Kobold Warren

- The party heads North into the great hall of the Kobold warren, (except **Izabella**, who is locked out by **Pen**,  and finds themselves intruding upon a union meeting of Kobold warriors. **Kerev** and **Trapmaster Travis** are not present and the warriors are unsure/ uneasy on how to treat the party.
- The Kobold warriors appear to have no idea how unions are meant to function. They are attempting to establish a charter of sorts but lack any understanding of the purpose of a union and have trouble codifying their structure because they also don't know how to run an effective meeting.
- **Syl** begins to lie and bargain with the union members
	- They convince the union members that their best bet for union leader is a full -fledged dragon, and that they (as a master recruiter and marketer) have already brought a red dragon into the fold.
- Tensions flair as **Pen** refuses to allow **Izabella** into the room. The Kobolds begin to doubt the veracity of **Syl**'s claims.
- A Kobold breaks down the door and the Kobolds are amazed by **Izabella**.
- Tensions flair (again) as **Izabella** dismisses the idea of a union, calls **Syl** a liar, demands the Kobolds pledge fealty to her.
	- There is a great deal of throat clearing somehow resulting in the Kobolds believing that monarchy is a valid structure for a union, so long as the old boss is taken down.
	- Not going to lie I'm not sure how we got here either. Kobolds are remarkably stupid.
	- The Kobolds pleadge fealty to **Izabella**

### Scene 5: Dragonkeeper

- The Kobolds march the party up to the dragonkeeper's den where **Kobold Boss Zolgran** and two loyal Kobold Scouts attack them.
- The party quickly slay **Zolgran** after which both scouts immediately declare their intention to join the union. 
- There is some painful discussion on the manner in which union dues work, and it's quickly settled that a declaration of unshakable fealty to **Izabella** counts as "paying dues."
- The Kobolds reveal that there are Zulgath in the "smelly room" and that they guard treasure. Zulgath are lizard-kin humanoids who sweat fish oil. They are amphibous, despite being lizards and also men. That's the way it is and you'll deal with it. 
- The Kobolds also reveal that the dragon is especially dangerous because it has not been fed recently. **Izabella** wants to deal with the "Beast" immediately and head back to the zulgath after resolving their current quest. Several party members want to dispatch the Zulgath first, especially **Pen** who believes that the Zulgath could satiate the dragon's hunger.
- The Kobolds do not want to confront the Zulgath at all because they smell terrible, but (after some confusion on voting) unanimously vote the way that New Boss **Izabella** instructs them to.
- The party learns to heal using *Treat Wounds*. They also learn that healing with short rests is not a thing.
- The 6 Kobold Warriors and 2 Kobold scouts appear to beleve that reasoning with the Beast is impossible and that it will have to be put down. They lead the party into the **Mushroom Groto** where the Beast is kept and fed.

### Scene 5: Mushroom Grotto

- The party cathes the attention of the "**Beast**" - a Juvenille Horned Dragon who appears to possess roughly the intellegence of a Mastiff. The party attempts to communicate with it. It does not communicate back.
- After **Izabella** allows the wyrmling to sniff her for a while, she turns into her dragon form, perhaps hoping something good would happen. The wyrmling sees her as a rival and attacks without hesitation. 
- The party struggles against the Beast's breath weapon (beltched poison). **Syl** is downed fairly quickly. **Kal** is able to carry them off to hide in a corner, and they are healed by a scout.
- There is a protracted 5 round battle. The party desperately attempts to convince the Kobolds to use non-lethal damage to down the dragon. The Kobolds seemingly have no understanding of what that means and deal significant damage using spears and crossbows. 
- The party asks **Izabella** to call off the Kobolds alltogether, and have them retreat to avoid killing the wyrmling. She has them stop attacking, but stay put.
	- A kobold dies in agony
- Finally, **Hymars** punches the wyrmling to sleep.
